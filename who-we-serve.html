Who We Serve

Find your situation

You do not need a label or a diagnosis to contact us. If something below sounds like your life right now, we can help. All of it is free and confidential.

  • Divorce or family breakdown

  • Gender-based violence

  • Protecting a child

  • In prison

  • Sex workers facing violence or abuse

  • Developmental trauma or chronic mental illness

Divorce or family breakdown

You are going through a divorce, or your family is coming apart

Separation changes everything at once: where you live, your money, your children, your sense of yourself. We help you handle the legal side and the human side together.

Legal help

  • Advice on your rights in marriage, divorce, property, and maintenance
  • Mediation, where it is safe and both people are willing
  • Representation in court when agreement is not possible
  • Custody and access arrangements that keep children at the centre

Psychological care

  • Individual therapy to steady yourself through the change
  • Couples or family therapy, including help to separate with less harm
  • Therapy for your children, so they have their own space to talk
  • Support before and after hard days in court

Gender-based violence

You have experienced violence or abuse

Whatever has happened, and whoever did it, you have the right to safety and to be heard. We work at your pace. You decide what steps to take and when.

Legal help

  • Protection orders and other legal measures to keep you safe
  • Support to make a criminal complaint, if you choose to
  • Representation through every stage of the legal process
  • Advice on divorce, custody, and property where violence is part of the picture

Psychological care

  • Trauma-informed therapy with a psychologist trained in this work
  • Help to prepare for interviews, statements, and hearings
  • Care that continues after the legal process ends
  • Therapy for children who have witnessed violence

Protecting a child

You are trying to protect a child

A child in danger, a custody fight, a guardian who has died: these are frightening situations for the adults and for the child. We work for the child's safety and wellbeing, and support the adults who are caring for them.

Legal help

  • Custody, access, and guardianship cases
  • Child protection measures where a child is at risk
  • Representing the child's interests before courts and authorities
  • Advice for parents, grandparents, and other carers

Psychological care

  • Therapy for children, designed for their age
  • Support for parents and carers under strain
  • Preparation for children who must speak to a court or official
  • Family therapy to rebuild stability after a crisis

In prison

You are in prison, or someone you love is

Being incarcerated does not take away your rights, or your need for care. Our lawyers and psychologists come into prisons and work with people there. Family members can contact us on behalf of someone inside.

Legal help

  • Legal advice and representation on your case and your conditions
  • Human rights protection for people in detention
  • Family law matters that continue while you are inside, such as custody and divorce
  • Advocacy with prison authorities on your behalf

Psychological care

  • Individual therapy inside the prison
  • Treatment for trauma, including trauma from before or during detention
  • Care for ongoing mental health conditions
  • Support for families outside

Sex workers facing violence or abuse

You are a sex worker and you have faced violence or abuse

Your rights are the same as anyone's. We do not judge how you earn your living. We help you use the law to protect yourself, and we care for the harm that has been done to you.

Legal help

  • Legal protection after violence, theft, or abuse by anyone, including officials
  • Support to make complaints safely, if you choose to
  • Advice on your children, housing, and family matters
  • Human rights advocacy on your behalf

Psychological care

  • Trauma-informed therapy in a place where you will not be judged
  • Care that fits around your life and your safety
  • Support for children, where relevant
  • Ongoing mental health care if you need it

Developmental trauma or chronic mental illness

You live with developmental trauma or a long-term mental illness

Early harm and long-term mental illness shape daily life. They can also make it harder to protect your rights, keep your home, or stay connected to your family. We offer continuing care, and a lawyer when you need one.

Legal help

  • Protection of your legal rights, including in family, housing, and guardianship matters
  • Representation when decisions are being made about you
  • Advocacy with institutions and authorities
  • Support for family members and carers on legal questions

Psychological care

  • Treatment for developmental trauma disorder
  • Continuing care for chronic mental health conditions
  • Home-based sessions if travel is difficult
  • Family therapy to strengthen the support around you

All our services are free of charge. We never ask the people we help to pay for anything. Legal aid, therapy, home visits, prison visits, and the hotline all cost nothing.

The Balance OrganizationWhere the law meets psychology

An Ethiopian non-profit in Addis Ababa. We provide free legal aid and psychological care together, so that no one has to choose between justice and healing.

We are not an emergency service. Our lines are answered during office hours, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm.
