You are in the right place.

Help here is free. What you tell us stays private. You can contact us in the way that feels easiest for you.

Send a request

What happens when you contact us

  1. You reach us

    Use the form below, call, or use the hotline. Say as much or as little as you want.

  2. We call you back

    Usually within two working days. We listen. We ask a few simple questions.

  3. We make a plan with you

    You meet a lawyer and a counsellor. They work together. You decide the pace.

What it costs

Nothing.

Legal help, therapy, home visits, and the hotline are all free. We will never ask you for money.

Who can use it

  • Anyone in need of therapy
  • Anyone going through divorce or family breakdown
  • Anyone trying to protect a child
  • Anyone who has experienced gender-based violence
  • People in prison or facing imprisonment, and their families
  • Anyone unable to travel, for any reason

Not sure? Contact us anyway.

Our promise to you

What you tell us stays with us. We do not share your name or your story with anyone without your permission.

The only exception is if the law requires us to act to keep a child or a life safe. If that ever happens, we will tell you.

You can stop at any time. You can ask us to delete your details.
