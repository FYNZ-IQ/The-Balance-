About The Balance Organization

Why we exist

Mental health is not a luxury. It is not an afterthought. It is a human right. Yet in Ethiopia today, thousands wake up with legal rights on paper but no one to heal their minds. They win their cases but lose themselves. They are declared free but remain imprisoned by their minds.

The Balance Organization exists because a right you cannot feel is not a right. A freedom you cannot enjoy is not freedom.

As Ethiopia's first integrated centre of its kind, The Balance Organization brings together mental health care and human rights protection.

Our core belief is simple: mental health is a human right, human rights are indivisible, and you cannot defend a person's rights while ignoring their mind. A legal victory without psychological healing does not give peace of mind. Healing without legal protection is not safety.

One place. Two kinds of help.

You never get one without the other

We are the first organization in Ethiopia to fully bring together human rights and mental health protection in the same place. Every client we work with has a lawyer and a psychologist assigned to their case.

Legal help

  • Human rights protection and litigation
  • Legal representation in any human rights case
  • Legal advocacy for survivors of gender-based violence
  • Child protection and custody advocacy

Why both

Because a right you cannot feel is not a right. A freedom you cannot enjoy is not freedom.

So we do not separate them. Your lawyer and your psychologist plan together, meet you together when it helps, and stay with you through the whole process.

Psychological care

  • Individual therapy
  • Couples therapy
  • Family therapy
  • Children's therapy
  • Mental health advocacy

Who we are

Where the law meets psychology

The Balance Organization is an Ethiopian non-governmental, non-profit organization based in Addis Ababa. We are the first centre in Ethiopia to fully integrate human rights protection with mental health advocacy, completely free of charge.

Most services in Ethiopia offer one or the other. We offer both, at the same time, from the same team. A lawyer and a psychologist plan every case together, to offer you a quality of life where your rights are respected and your peace of mind is served.
