Impact & Reports

What we do, counted honestly

We report what we can measure and we say what we cannot. We do not publish success rates or case outcomes, because every case is different and because the people in them are not statistics.

What we measure

The numbers we track

  • People who contacted us, and how (office, home, prison, hotline)
  • People who received integrated help, by service area
  • Legal consultations, court appearances, and mediations
  • Therapy sessions, by type: individual, couples and family, children
  • Home visits and prison visits
  • Hotline calls by region
  • Volunteer professionals trained and hours contributed
  • Human rights cases documented and policy submissions made
How we report

Regular, audited, public

Quarterly

A short update on activity in each service area, shared with donors and published here.

Annually

A full report: what we did, what we learned, how we spent every birr, and what we will change.

Independently audited

Our accounts are audited each year by an independent auditor. The audit is published alongside the annual report.

What we do not publish

Our limits, on purpose

No client stories

We do not publish the stories, photographs, or names of the people we work with. Their privacy matters more than our publicity.

No success rates

A court outcome is not a measure of a person's safety or healing. We report what we did, not a score.

Support this work

Everything we report on is free to the people receiving it. Donations make that possible.

Donate

All our services are free of charge. We never ask the people we help to pay for anything. Legal aid, therapy, home visits, prison visits, and the hotline all cost nothing.

The Balance OrganizationWhere the law meets psychology

An Ethiopian non-profit in Addis Ababa. We provide free legal aid and psychological care together, so that no one has to choose between justice and healing.

We are not an emergency service. Our lines are answered during office hours, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm.
