Financial transparency

How we account for your money

  • Our accounts are audited each year by an independent auditor. We publish the audit in full.
  • We publish an annual report showing income, spending by programme, and what the money paid for.
  • Designated gifts are tracked separately and spent only on the fund you chose.
  • We never charge the people we help. Every donation goes toward services, not toward covering what clients would otherwise pay.
  • Every donor receives a receipt. Regular donors receive a short update each quarter.

Other ways to give

Organisations, law firms, and foundations can fund a whole programme, second staff, or provide in-kind support such as office space or transport. Write to help@thebalance-ethiopia.org.

Give your time instead

Licensed lawyers and psychologists can take on a small pro bono caseload with training and supervision.

Volunteer

All our services are free of charge. We never ask the people we help to pay for anything. Legal aid, therapy, home visits, prison visits, and the hotline all cost nothing.

The Balance OrganizationWhere the law meets psychology

An Ethiopian non-profit in Addis Ababa. We provide free legal aid and psychological care together, so that no one has to choose between justice and healing.

We are not an emergency service. Our lines are answered during office hours, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm.
